## A few things I've made

**[Hera Space Companion](https://giuseppegalilei.com/projects/hera-companion/)**  
An AI experience for exploring ESA’s Hera mission through conversation, mission imagery and interactive spacecraft views. I built it through Impact AI, working with Markus Mooslechner of Terra Mater Studios and the wider project team.

**[Pyna](https://giuseppegalilei.com/projects/pyna/)**  
A team-built student experiment connecting computer vision, music and lighting. From a hackathon prototype to Maker Faire Turin and Rome—and a lesson in the difference between recognising an expression and understanding a mood.

**[WAP — With A Purpose](https://giuseppegalilei.com/projects/wap/)**  
A browser-extension prototype connecting news stories with relevant NGOs. Built with a multicultural team at the European Innovation Academy.
